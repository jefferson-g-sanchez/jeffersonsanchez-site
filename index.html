About

Jefferson Sanchez is an MIT-educated entrepreneur and engineer at the intersection of construction technology and artificial intelligence. He is co-founder and COO of Bimrock AI, developing AI-powered BIM automation tools for MEP engineers, and a contributor to The 3D Web Coder. With a background spanning environmental research, sustainable design, and multiple venture builds, Jefferson brings a systems-level perspective to one of the world's oldest industries.
