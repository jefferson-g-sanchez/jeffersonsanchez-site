About

Jefferson G. Sanchez is a builder at the intersection of AI, sustainability, and the built environment. A graduate of MIT and the University of Michigan, he holds a LEED AP credential and has founded multiple ventures in construction technology — including Revonate, Better Builders, Refresh Construction, and Torque AI. Today, he leads Bimrock AI, developing AI-powered BIM automation tools that help MEP engineers work faster and smarter. His work is rooted in the belief that software can fundamentally reshape how we design and build the world.
